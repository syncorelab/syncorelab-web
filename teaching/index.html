Signal detection · General Psychology I

Die Nachtklappe

This demonstration is in German

You are the cat flap. The cat comes home at night, sometimes with prey in its mouth, sometimes with nothing but a leaf. The camera image is blurry and time is short. Over three nights you collect hits and false alarms, and from them sensitivity and criterion.

The demonstration separates two things that merge in intuition: how well you can tell things apart, and how cautiously you decide. A better camera shifts the one, rain and hunting season shift the other, and you notice it in your own numbers before any formula appears.
